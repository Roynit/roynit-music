## Shop art

The Orbs shop's pictures (assets/shop) are drawn by scripts/shop_assets.py. Some decorations are built from silhouettes on https://game-icons.net, used under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/): icons made by Lorc (feathered wing, snowflakes, paw print) and Delapouite (musical notes, tiara). The silhouettes are recoloured, shaded and arranged; the original SVGs are kept in assets/shop/_src/game-icons.
